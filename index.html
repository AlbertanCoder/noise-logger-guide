What this is

A box that listens all day and writes down when noise happens, how long, how loud and what kind of sound, so there is an honest dated record to show someone.

On day one it should

  • Log every event: date, time, length, loudness compared with your quiet room, and likely sound types.
  • Count the night ones (10 pm to 7 am), make a report, and open on your phone.
  • Let you tap Mine, Theirs or Not sure on an event.

The log software passes its tests on a laptop and has not run on a Pi yet. Two faults must be fixed before day one: long, steady noise can be missed, and event times can drift.

It does not

  • Act as a legal sound meter. Decibels will be estimates, and only a bylaw officer can confirm a violation.
  • Know whose noise it is. That waits until a learning test passes; until then every event says the source is not decided.
  • Keep conversations. Audio is off. No filter is perfect, so thump clips are tested before they can be turned on.
  • Play any sound. There is no speaker.

Later, in this order

  1. Decibel estimates from the mic's calibration file.
  2. Short thump clips, never when talking is heard.
  3. A small sound model that learns whose noise it is from your taps. It counts only if it passes its test.

It could also be used for

  • A portfolio piece: this guide, the model and its checks.
  • A sleep record: what woke you and when.
  • Loud noise from next door.

None of the "later" or "also" items is built, except the tap buttons.
